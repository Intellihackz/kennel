# Kennel brand

Generated by `scripts/brand.mjs` (run after `npm run build` with the site served; it needs Playwright). The wordmark outline comes from `scripts/outline-wordmark.py`.

## Idea

A kennel is a private place where you train something that is yours. The picture for it is the workshop from the site: tokens go in as crates, an agent is built, and it ends up inside a locked glass room.

## Mark

An isometric cube outline (the room) with a small amber cube inside (the agent). Cube outline in ink or ground, inner cube always amber. It reads at 16px and survives a circular crop. Do not fill the outline, do not add a lock, and do not recolour the inner cube: amber means "the agent" everywhere in the product.

## Wordmark

`kennel`, lowercase, Unbounded ExtraBold (800), tracking -0.04em, outlined to paths so it needs no font. The lockup is the mark followed by the wordmark, mark about 1.25 times the letter height.

## Voice

Short and literal. Say tokens, not addresses. Say "train", "test", "room". Five tokens is the minimum to unlock an agent, never the amount needed to train it. No promises about returns, ever.
